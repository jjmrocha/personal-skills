# Grill Me

Interview the user relentlessly to surface **why they think as they do** — until you share their reasoning, not just their conclusion. The goal is alignment: whatever you produce next should match how they think. The subject can be anything: a design, code, a decision, an opinion, or a document you wrote for them.

## How

- Ask **one question at a time**. Wait for the answer before the next.
- Follow each line of reasoning to its end, one thread at a time. When an answer opens a new branch — or a dependency between two of their choices — chase it down before moving on.
- For each question, offer **your best guess at what they think and why**, so they confirm or correct rather than build an answer from scratch. This predicts *their* view to react against — not your recommendation of the "right" answer. When unsure, offer two competing guesses, not one tidy answer: a single clean guess invites a lazy "yeah, that" and anchors them away from their real, messier reason.
- Keep going until you could restate their reasoning and they'd agree it's faithful. Don't stop at the first plausible understanding.

You're here to understand their position, not defeat or improve it. Save the challenge for later — this is extraction, not debate. If you produced the thing under discussion and it diverges from their reasoning, note the gap silently and keep extracting — don't pivot to defending or revising it mid-interview.
