# grill-me

Get interviewed about your own thinking until the agent actually shares your reasoning.

## What it does

Turns the usual dynamic around: instead of the agent proposing and you reacting, it questions you — one question at a time — about *why* you think what you think. The point is that whatever gets built next matches how you'd have built it, not how the model would have.

Each question comes with the agent's best guess at your answer, so you confirm or correct rather than composing from scratch. When it's genuinely unsure it offers two competing guesses instead of one tidy one — a single clean guess invites a lazy "yeah, that" and pulls you away from your real, messier reason.

## When it triggers

"Grill me", "interview me", "make sure you understand why I think this" — or any point where you want intent extracted before work starts. The subject can be a design, a code approach, a decision, an opinion, or a document the agent wrote for you.

## What it deliberately isn't

Not a debate. The agent is extracting your position, not defeating or improving it. If it wrote the thing under discussion and your reasoning contradicts it, it notes the gap silently and keeps asking — no mid-interview pivot to defending its own draft.

## Pairs with

Run it before planning or implementation, so the plan inherits your reasoning instead of the model's defaults.
